# Vínculos institucionales, capacidades y ecosistema de aliados de la Universidad del Pacífico (UP, Lima, Perú — up.edu.pe)

**Scope note for the report writer:** All findings refer ONLY to Universidad del Pacífico (Jr. Sánchez Cerro 2141, Jesús María, Lima — up.edu.pe). Search results for "Universidad del Pacífico" of Chile (closed 2019–2021), Ecuador, Paraguay and Colombia were reviewed and explicitly excluded. Method note: www.up.edu.pe returns HTTP 403 to automated fetching, so official UP URLs below were located via Brave Search / Wayback Machine CDX indexes and (where noted) their snippet content was read; pages on subdomains (ciup.up.edu.pe, ori.up.edu.pe, cechap.up.edu.pe, agenda2026.up.edu.pe, admision.up.edu.pe) and PDFs were fetched in full and are the strongest sources. ## ¿Qué alianzas, convenios y asociaciones de investigación formales tiene la UP con el Estado, cámaras/gremios, multilaterales y empresas?

### Takeaway
UP has a documented but modestly publicized portfolio of framework agreements with national public bodies (Cancillería/MRE, Tribunal de Justicia de la CAN), a large volume of academic agreements with universities, and a research track record in which CIUP investigators have worked with the World Bank, IDB, CAF, UN Environment, GIZ, USAID, SDC, IDRC and major US foundations — but no public, consolidated registry of agreements with ministries, sector chambers (confederaciones gremiales) or multilaterals was found, and no BID/PNUD/CEPLAN/OCDE project page naming UP as institutional partner was located.

## ¿Existen centros o programas que conecten investigación con práctica (observatorios, consultoría, incubadoras, cátedras, unidades de innovación, extensión)?

### Takeaway
Yes — UP's bridge infrastructure is centered on CIUP (a nationally recognized think tank that explicitly includes consulting as a university function), four specialized centers (CECHAP, CEMS, CEGES/CERS, Centro Cultural), the Escuela de Gestión Pública (EGP), an IDRC-funded cátedra, policy-offering initiatives (Agenda 2026, CeCo Perú, "Punto de Equilibrio"), and a US$18–20M innovation hub under construction for 2027.

### Cited Findings
- **CIUP (Centro de Investigación de la Universidad del Pacífico)**: "la unidad interdepartamental e interfacultativa a través de la cual la universidad propicia, realiza y difunde **la investigación y la consultoría como funciones propias**"; founded **1971** per UP history (Wikipedia) but **1972** per CIUP's own site and the VRI brochure — [Wikipedia (es)](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_del_Pac%C3%ADfico_(Per%C3%BA)) ; [CIUP "Nosotros"](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/nosotros/) ; [VRI brochure p.14](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf). **(Founding-year discrepancy 1971 vs 1972 — note it.)**
- CIUP self-describes as "**uno de los principales think tanks del Perú**" whose proposals "influyen con evidencias en las políticas públicas", and states that "muchos de sus investigadores han ocupado altos cargos en el Estado, así como en directorios y consejos consultivos de entidades privadas" — [CIUP Nosotros](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/nosotros/)
- **Award**: CIUP has won the **Premio PODER a la mejor think tank del Perú since 2013** (areas: economic policy & finance, social policies & citizenship; awarded by Revista Poder and On Think Tanks) — [VRI brochure p.15](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf)
- **11 interdisciplinary research areas** at CIUP including *Regulación, Infraestructura y Competencia*; *Políticas Sociales, Desarrollo y Sociedad*; *Tecnologías de Información e Innovación*; *Emprendimiento e Innovación*; *Gestión Empresarial y Responsabilidad Social* — [CIUP Nosotros](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/nosotros/). The website's topic taxonomy includes **"Gestión pública"** and "Política y Gobierno" (corrupción, legislación, relaciones internacionales) — directly aligned with a governance/public-management proposal — [ciup.up.edu.pe temas](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/temas/politica-y-gobierno/gestion-publica/)
- **Policy-transfer initiatives run by CIUP**: "Laboratorio de Inteligencia Artificial para la toma de decisiones", "Agenda Perú – Regional", "Agenda Perú Bicentenario", "CeCo Perú" — [VRI brochure p.15](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf)
- **Agenda 2026**: a CIUP project "en colaboración con la **Escuela de Gestión Pública (EGP) de la Universidad del Pacífico**", producing **15 detailed national public-policy proposals** (security, fiscal policy, human capital, employment, environmental sustainability) explicitly aimed at future government plans and implementation strategies — [agenda2026.up.edu.pe](https://agenda2026.up.edu.pe/) ; press page: [agenda2026.up.edu.pe/prensa](https://agenda2026.up.edu.pe/prensa/)
- **Agenda 2026 Regional**: 11 proposals for regional and local governments, presented by CIUP "en alianza con El Comercio" — [CIUP/Instagram post describing the alliance](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dee-86Wjn_Q/) *(secondary/weak source — corroborate before citing)*; El Comercio's rector interview independently confirms Agenda 2026 as UP's evidence-based policy-proposal vehicle — [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/economia/universidad-del-pacifico-tendra-un-hub-de-innovacion-en-el-2027-cual-sera-la-inversion-y-proyecciones-con-este-proyecto-noticia/)
- **Seminars / dissemination series**: "Seminarios CIUP" and the recurring analysis/boletín "**Punto de Equilibrio**" (e.g., N°66, Aug 2026, on the first-100-days government agenda, filed under *Gestión pública*) — [ciup.up.edu.pe/seminarios](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/seminarios/) ; [example Punto de Equilibrio N°66](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/analisis/punto-de-equilibrio-n66-el-pais-no-puede-esperar-la-agenda-de-los-primeros-100-dias/)
- **Observatorio-type output**: "Observatorio: Estado del mercado voluntario de carbono peruano 2024" (CIUP book/publication) — [CIUP publicaciones](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/publicaciones/) *(a single verified example; no standing "observatorio" unit found)*
- **CECHAP (Centro de Estudios sobre China y Asia-Pacífico, founded 2013)**: only independent academic center on contemporary China in Peru; acts as **APEC Study Centre**; member of the **APEC Study Centre Consortium** and the **Fudan-Latin America University Consortium**; member of the **Red Peruana para Estudios del Asia-Pacífico**; joint research with the Institute of World Economics and Politics of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences — [VRI brochure p.18](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf) ; [cechap.up.edu.pe](https://cechap.up.edu.pe/)
- **CEMS (Centro de Estudios sobre Minería y Sostenibilidad)**: explicitly aims to become a national/international reference for **consultoría (analysis + proposed solutions) and capacitación** on mining; its international partners include **GIZ, USAID, IDB and Oxford University**; mission is to integrate State, companies and communities via public policy and business management — [VRI brochure p.19](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf) ; [cems.up.edu.pe](https://cems.up.edu.pe)
- **CEGES / CERS (ethics & sustainability center)**: promotes multi-actor dialogue "para influir en los líderes del sector público, privado y de la sociedad civil" around the SDGs; participates in AUSJAL working groups on University Social Responsibility and Environment/Sustainability — [VRI brochure p.20](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf). **Naming discrepancy**: VRI brochure/Wikipedia call it *Centro de Ética y Gestión Sostenible (CEGES)* at ceges.up.edu.pe, while CIUP's site links *Centro de Ética y Responsabilidad Social (CERS)* at cers.up.edu.pe — [CIUP Nosotros](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/nosotros/)
- **Escuela de Gestión Pública (EGP)** — a dedicated UP school bridging to public management: specialization programs such as *Gestión y Finanzas Públicas* whose faculty bios show serving officials/ex-officials (e.g., former directors at MIDIS and MINEDU; MEF technical-assistance program coordinators; directors at SUNASS; consultants to OSIPTEL, GIZ, BID, OSITRAN, MEF, MTC) — [UP EGP, plana docente](https://www.up.edu.pe/egp/programas-especializacion/gestion-finanzas-publicas/plana-docente/) ; EGP co-authors Agenda 2026 with CIUP — [agenda2026.up.edu.pe](https://agenda2026.up.edu.pe/)
- **Cátedra**: Cátedra de Investigación en Migraciones y Desplazamientos Forzados (2022–2026, IDRC-funded) hosting research, dissemination, policy-incidence (incidencia en políticas públicas), teaching and network-building — [VRI brochure p.9](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf)
- **Innovation infrastructure**: UP will build a **hub de innovación** (US$18–20M investment): first stone January 2026, operational **first quarter of 2027** (UP's 65th anniversary); it will **integrate all laboratories of its 12 programs**, serve students first and also external users/clients, and be located adjacent to the building housing the Centro de Investigación — rector Martha Chávez Passano, interviewed at CADE Ejecutivos 2025 — [El Comercio, 10/12/2025](https://elcomercio.pe/economia/universidad-del-pacifico-tendra-un-hub-de-innovacion-en-el-2027-cual-sera-la-inversion-y-proyecciones-con-este-proyecto-noticia/)
- **Entrepreneurship**: UP maintains an entrepreneurship portal (emprendeup.pe) linked from its official navigation — [link listed on ori.up.edu.pe](http://emprendeup.pe/) *(existence only; contents not reviewed)*
- **Historical extension/consulting arms** (useful for the proposal's institutional history): Centro de Consultoría en Gestión Empresarial (created 1973), Centro de Desarrollo Gerencial (1987), Centro de Gestión y Desarrollo de Negocios (CGDN, 2000) — [Wikipedia (es) history section](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_del_Pac%C3%ADfico_(Per%C3%BA))
- **Postgraduate programs adjacent to the proposal's themes**: UP's Escuela de Postgrado offers masters in **Gestión Pública**, **Regulación de Servicios Públicos y Gestión de Infraestructura**, **Gestión de la Inversión Social**, **Derecho Administrativo Económico**, Supply Chain Management — [Wikipedia (es)](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_del_Pac%C3%ADfico_(Per%C3%BA)) *(secondary source; UP postgrado pages not fetched)*

### Inferences
- EGP + CIUP + Agenda 2026 form a ready-made "research→policy" pipeline for a proposal on innovation, value chains, governance and public management; CEMS and the Regulation/Infrastructure/Competencia area supply the regulatory/value-chain angle.
- The 2027 innovation hub, physically adjacent to CIUP, signals institutional intent to formalize industry-facing applied work (labs as services to externals).

### Gaps
- **No incubator/accelerator or business-roundtable (feria/rueda de negocios) run by UP was found**; emprendeup.pe contents not reviewed.
- Current status of the historic consulting centers (Centro de Consultoría en Gestión Empresarial, CDG, CGDN) could not be confirmed on today's UP website — they may have been absorbed into CIUP or the EGP.
- No current institutional "unidad de innovación" web page was found (only the El Comercio announcement of the future hub).

## ¿Cuál es el registro de la UP en consultorías y asistencia técnica (informes, asesorías a ministerios, municipios, reguladores)?

### Takeaway
The record is documented mainly at two levels: (1) institutional — CIUP formally bundles "investigación y consultoría," produces "estudios e informes," and runs policy-proposal initiatives, and CEMS is mandated to offer consultancy in mining with GIZ/USAID/IDB/Oxford partners; (2) individual — UP faculty CVs list consultancy for the World Bank, IDB, DFID, European Commission, CAN, MEF, MINEDU, MIDIS, OSIPTEL, OSITRAN, SUNASS, MTC and GIZ. A consolidated public registry of UP contracts with ministries/municipalities/regulators was not found.

### Cited Findings
- **Institutional mandate**: CIUP "propicia, realiza y difunde la investigación y **la consultoría** como funciones propias" — [Wikipedia (es), citing UP's "Sobre el CIUP"](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_del_Pac%C3%ADfico_(Per%C3%BA))
- **CIUP publication types include "Estudio e informe"** (e.g., "El efecto Chancay: cambios en la carga, el comercio y la red de impuestos", 2025) — [CIUP publicaciones](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/publicaciones/)

### Inferences
- The practical advisory vehicle for a proposal would be CIUP (research contracts/studies) plus the EGP (technical assistance/capacity-building for public officials), with CEMS as a sectoral model of how UP structures consultancy mandates.

### Gaps

## ¿A qué redes académicas internacionales pertenece la UP (consorcios, redes de investigación, afiliaciones), especialmente en economía, gestión, gobernanza e innovación?

### Takeaway
UP (and CIUP in particular) sits in a broad, verifiable network stack: business-school accreditations and associations (AACSB, AMBA, PRME), Jesuit and Ibero-American university networks (AUSJAL, CLADEA), research networks in economics/management (LACEA, LASA, AOM, MIT SCALE, POMS, SEKN), Asia-Pacific/APEC networks through CECHAP, and 130–150+ bilateral university exchange agreements plus double-degree programs.

## ¿Qué infraestructura de investigación existe (semillas/fondos, concursos internos, revistas, seminarios, apoyo doctoral)?

### Takeaway
UP has a mature, well-documented research-support system managed by the Vicerrectorado de Investigación since 2017: annual internal research competitions for faculty and students (the functional equivalent of "semillas"), dedicated funds (internationalization, Oxford-linked Rose Mary Thorp fund, travel, translation, publication prizes, sabbatical), a CRIS platform (FacultyUP) aligned with CONCYTEC's #PerúCRIS, a research data repository (DatasetsUP), two indexed journals, a large editorial/library/repository stack, ~95 funded projects per year, and a research-assistant pipeline — but no program explicitly labeled "semillas de investigación" and no clearly documented standalone doctoral program were found.

### Cited Findings
- **VRI incentive instruments in place since 2017** (verbatim list): "Concurso anual de investigación para profesores; Concurso anual de investigación para alumnos; Fondo de internacionalización de la investigación; **Fondo Rose Mary Thorp – University of Oxford**; Premios por publicar en revistas indizadas y editoriales de prestigio; Fondo de viajes para presentación de trabajos académicos (profesores; alumnos y asistentes); Fondo para la traducción y edición de trabajos académicos; Política de sabático; **Fondo de promoción para la gestión de fuentes de financiamiento de investigación**" — [VRI brochure pp.5–6](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf)
- **Scale of research activity**: "+95 Proyectos de investigación anuales, en promedio, en los últimos 5 años con fondos internos y externos"; +111 profesores investigadores e investigadores afiliados; +185 profesionales dedicados a la investigación; +75 asistentes de investigación (students and recent graduates); every full-time professor leads at least one research project per year — [VRI brochure p.14](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf)
- **Research culture metrics**: >90% of full-time professors dedicate half their time to research; >80% of researcher-professors hold a PhD, of which >80% obtained abroad — [VRI brochure p.4](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf)
- **CRIS / FacultyUP**: launched 2021, meets the worldwide CRIS standard and CONCYTEC's **#PerúCRIS** initiative; centralizes profiles, production, networks and links to **6,000+ publications** with institutional affiliation — [VRI brochure pp.7–8](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf) ; [faculty.up.edu.pe](http://faculty.up.edu.pe/)
- **Research data repository DatasetsUP** (Dataverse software), launched Sept 2022 by VRI + Library, to interconnect with the Institutional Repository and FacultyUP — [VRI brochure p.9](https://sisisemail.up.edu.pe/sisisemail/docs/2026/247/brochure-vri-espanol.pdf) ; [datasets.up.edu.pe](https://datasets.up.edu.pe/)

### Inferences

- "Dos doctorados compartidos" suggests cotutelle/shared doctoral pathways (likely with foreign partners), which would be the route for doctoral support of proposal staff — **inference, needs confirmation with the VRI**.
