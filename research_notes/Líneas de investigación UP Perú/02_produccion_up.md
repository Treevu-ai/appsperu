## ¿Qué revistas publica la UP? (alcance, indexación, temas recientes)

### Takeaway
UP (Lima) operates an OJS journal portal with **three journals** — *Apuntes. Revista de Ciencias Sociales* (CIUP, since 1973, **Scopus + ESCI + SciELO Perú + Latindex + Redalyc + DOAJ**), *Journal of Business* (School of Business; management/entrepreneurship/**innovation**/sustainability), and *Forseti. Revista de Derecho* (law alumni association). I found **no evidence of a currently published UP journal named "Economía"** on the portal — flagging this as a gap (see below).

### Cited Findings
- UP's **Portal de Revistas Académicas UP** (revistas.up.edu.pe) lists exactly three journals: **Apuntes. Social Sciences Journal**, **Journal of Business (Universidad del Pacífico, Lima, Peru)**, and **Forseti. Law Review** — [Portal de Revistas Académicas UP](https://revistas.up.edu.pe/index/en)
- **Apuntes. Revista de Ciencias Sociales**: biannual, edited/published by the Research Center of UP (CIUP) **since 1973**; scope = empirical social-science research on Latin America and Peru with emphasis on social policies; sections = original research articles + notes/reviews; Spanish issues with English versions from issue 73 onward — [Apuntes About](https://revistas.up.edu.pe/apuntes/en/about); [Portal](https://revistas.up.edu.pe/index/en)
- **Apuntes indexation (per its own About page)**: **Scopus**, **Emerging Sources Citation Index (ESCI)**, **SciELO Perú**, **DOAJ**, ProQuest (Social Sciences Journal Database, Prisma, Periodicals Index Online, IBSS), EBSCO (Fuente Académica Premier), Cengage, **Dialnet**, **Latindex**, Clase, **ERIH Plus**, HAPI, **REDIB**, WorldCat/Ulrich's; archived via CLOCKSS/Keepers Registry; CC BY 3.0; **no article processing charges** (covered by the UP Vice-Rectorate for Research) — [Apuntes About — Indexing](https://revistas.up.edu.pe/apuntes/en/about)
- Apuntes identifiers: **ISSN 0252-1865, eISSN 2223-1757, DOI 10.21678/0252-1865**; Scimago SJR 2025 = **0.296 (Q2)**, H-index 7 (Economics / Social Sciences subject areas) — [Apuntes About](https://revistas.up.edu.pe/apuntes/en/about); [Scimago record](https://www.scimagojr.com/journalsearch.php?q=21100927369&tip=sid&clean=0)
- Apuntes was **indexed in Redalyc** (announced by CIUP): "Apuntes. Revista de Ciencias Sociales recibió el certificado de indexación a la Red de revistas científicas de Acceso Abierto (Redalyc)" — [CIUP news](https://ciup.up.edu.pe/analisis/apuntes-es-indexada-a-redalyc-red-de-revistas-cientificas-de-acceso-abierto/)
- **Journal of Business** (published by UP's School of Business): aims to disseminate theoretical and applied research; main areas = **management, leadership, entrepreneurship, innovation, sustainability, corporate social responsibility, business ethics** — [Journal page](https://revistas.up.edu.pe/business/en)
- **Forseti. Revista de Derecho**: biannual, free-access, edited by the UP Law School alumni association (Derup Editores); articles + jurisprudence/administrative rulings — [Journal page](https://revistas.up.edu.pe/forseti/en)
- Portal framing: "The Universidad del Pacífico promotes interdisciplinary research in economics, as well as in social and business sciences… *Apuntes* has been at the heart of the UP Research Center's activities for more than 40 years" — [Portal](https://revistas.up.edu.pe/index/en)

### Inferences
- The **"Economía" revista** appears in UP's internal Pure system (FacultyUP) as an activity of the Departamento Académico de Economía / CIUP with professors acting as reviewers (e.g., F. Galarza Arellano), but it is **not listed on UP's current journals portal** — [FacultyUP: Economía (Revista)](https://faculty.up.edu.pe/es/activities/econom%C3%ADa-revista/). I could not confirm whether it still publishes new issues (see Gaps).
- Apuntes is clearly UP's flagship indexed outlet and is the natural home for empirical work on Peruvian value chains, governance and public policy; *Journal of Business* is the natural home for innovation/entrepreneurship work.

### Gaps
- **Could not verify** current publication status/last issue of the journal "Economía" (UP, Lima) or its indexation (Latindex/SciELO) — needs a direct check of its old URL/Latindex catalogue.
- **Could not verify** indexation status of *Journal of Business* and *Forseti* (not stated on the portal landing pages; I did not fetch their About pages).
- SciELO Perú inclusion is asserted by UP's own About page; I could not independently open SciELO's journal catalogue to confirm it in this session (SciELO does index Apuntes historically — treat as UP-asserted + widely corroborated, but unverified here).

## Proyectos de investigación recientes (2019–2026) y financiamiento

### Takeaway
CIUP's public project registry shows **55 research projects**, but they are dated ~2018 (registry entry dates), so the registry itself is stale; the clearest *funded, recent, thematic* evidence is **two ProCIENCIA/CONCYTEC-funded projects (2024, S/ 98,500 each)** — one on the **camelid South-American value chain** (Patricia Larios) and one on **lithium governance in Peru** (Roger Merino). CIUP also publishes commissioned impact-evaluation studies and annual observatory reports.
